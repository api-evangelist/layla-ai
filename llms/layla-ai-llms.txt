# Layla

> Layla (layla.ai) is an AI travel agent: you describe the trip you want in chat, and Layla plans the itinerary, prices and finds flights, hotels and activities, and helps you book. Layla is human-in-the-loop: a real human travel expert can review the plan, check feasibility, fine-tune the logistics and even book the trip for you at no extra cost. The site is available in English (default) and under the /es, /fr, /it, /zh, /de, /pt, /ru, /ar and /pl locale paths.

## Product

- [Layla — AI trip planner & travel agent](https://layla.ai/): Chat-based travel planning: personalized itineraries, flights, hotels and activities in one place.
- [Plan a trip with Layla](https://layla.ai/chat): The planning chat. Tell Layla where, when and how you like to travel, and it builds the trip with you.
- [Couples trip planner](https://layla.ai/plan/couple-trip-planner): Romantic trips made simple — custom itineraries, destinations and stays for two.
- [Family vacation planner](https://layla.ai/plan/family-vacation-trip-planner): Family trips planned end to end: itineraries, road trips, flights and hotels.
- [Roam Around](https://layla.ai/roamaround): Roam Around's AI travel itineraries are now part of Layla.
